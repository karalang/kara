One row per normative unit of docs/library/time-random-env.md, as of 2026-11-25, naming the cell in this directory that covers it. This is the existing `conformance/random_env` directory, which the README's cases.py line already runs: its 19 cells stay as they were; the 2026-11-20 io pass's time and env programs are moved here and renamed (`time_durations_instants_and_timeout_1120`, `time_duration_overflow_case*`, `env_set_invalid_case*`, `providers_env_clock_random_1120`, `time_fake_without_wall_ns_panics_1120`); the rest are new: the page's three programs, the clock's effects and its freedom from `par` conflicts, `timeout`'s drops and `?`, a fake that implements one clock method, `env.set` reaching a child, and a seeded generator charged nothing.
